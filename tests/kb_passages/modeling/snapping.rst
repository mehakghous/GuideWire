********
Snapping
********

Snapping makes a transform jump to useful points. Synthetic page for the passage-ranking test.

Target
======

The snap target chooses which part of the moving object lands on the destination, such as its center, median point or closest point.

Increment
=========

Increment snapping moves in fixed steps of grid units instead of landing on geometry.

Project
=======

Face projection snapping drops the moved element onto the surface of another mesh along the view direction.

Rotation
========

Snapping can also round a rotation to fixed angle steps, such as fifteen degrees.
