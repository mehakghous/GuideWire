***********
Inset Faces
***********

The inset tool creates new faces inside the selected faces. Synthetic page for the passage-ranking test.

Depth
=====

The depth option raises or lowers the new inner faces along the face normal, so the inset can form a pit or a boss.

Boundary
========

The boundary option keeps the outer border edges of the selection unchanged, which stops the inset from pulling in the surrounding geometry.

Outset
======

The outset option moves the new edges outward instead of inward, which enlarges the selected region.

Individual
==========

The individual option insets every selected face on its own, instead of treating the selection as one connected region.
